  2. Silver Build a tent: a box body plus a 4-sided cone roof, in two colors.
    Done when: the tent stands on the grass and the roof sits flush on the body.
  3. Gold Clicking a peg drops a ring that slides down onto it.
    Done when: every peg click drops a ring and the counter goes up.
  4. Bonus Pick one: drag to look around, upright cars, or a blinking bulb.
