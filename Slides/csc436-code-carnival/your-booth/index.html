Getting started (25 minutes)

  1. Pick an idea (below) in 2 minutes. Small and finished beats big and broken.
  2. Open your-booth/index.html (the page) and your-booth/booth.js (the 3D stage and code).
  3. Name your booth in the <h1> above. Save. The page updates by itself.
  4. Split up: one teammate per ticket. Every booth you visited today is a parts bin. Copy freely from ring-toss/main.js, fun-house/index.html, and safety-inspector/guestbook.js.
  5. Broken? Open the Console (Cmd+Option+J or Ctrl+Shift+J). At 5 minutes left, stop adding and fix what you have.

Booth Permit Requirements

  • Bronze One framework attraction: Tailwind styling, a DaisyUI component or theme, or an Animate.css effect. Copy from: the Fun House.
  • Silver One Three.js attraction in the stage below (a spinning prize, a ride, a game). Replace the placeholder gem in booth.js. Copy from: the Ring Toss (Ferris wheel, pegs, tent).
  • Gold One safety feature: a visitor input that is validated and rendered with textContent. Starter: uncomment the form below and the GOLD block in booth.js.
  • Bonus Make the 3D and CSS talk: clicking something in 3D changes the page, or a form changes the 3D scene. Starter: the BONUS block in booth.js.
