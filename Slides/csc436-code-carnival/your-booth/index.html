Booth Permit Requirements (25 minutes)

Do Step 0 first (booth name and team names in the header). Then earn as many permits as you can. Each one is checked by the "Done when" line.

  • Bronze One framework attraction. Pick ONE: a Tailwind-styled prize card (copy your Fun House Bronze card), a DaisyUI component or theme switcher (copy from the Fun House Silver section), or an Animate.css effect on click (copy funhouse.js). Put it inside <section id="attraction"> below.
    Done when: the volunteer can see it, and for Animate.css, it replays on every click.
  • Silver One Three.js attraction in the #stage. Open booth.js and replace the yellow placeholder with your own ride, game, or prize.
    Done when: the stage shows at least 2 meshes you made (not the placeholder) and at least one of them moves.
  • Gold One safety feature. The visitor form below is already built. Follow the GOLD steps in booth.js to validate it and show entries with textContent.
    Done when: a blank entry shows an error, an entry over 100 characters shows an error, and <b>hi</b> shows up as plain text with the tags visible.
  • Bonus Make the 3D and the page talk. Pick one recipe at the bottom of booth.js.
    Done when: clicking a 3D object changes the page, OR submitting the form changes the 3D scene.
